Our Expertise

What Struked LLC Provides

Each of our services is focused on giving construction professionals the accurate cost data and planning support they need to manage projects effectively.

Estimation

Construction Cost Estimation

Detailed construction cost estimates to help contractors plan project budgets and evaluate expected expenses. We review your project documents and prepare cost breakdowns that support sound decision-making before construction begins.

  • Review of project drawings and specifications
  • Itemized cost breakdown by trade or division
  • Material and quantity-based pricing analysis
  • Suitable for bidding and pre-construction planning

Takeoffs

Material Takeoffs

Accurate quantity calculations for construction materials based on project drawings and specifications. Our material takeoffs give you the data you need to procure the right quantities and avoid costly overages or shortages.

  • Quantity calculations from drawings and plans
  • Organized by material type and trade scope
  • Supports procurement and budget preparation
  • Available for various construction types

Design-Build

Design-Build Services

Integrated design and construction planning support to help coordinate project requirements. We assist in bridging the gap between design documentation and construction execution by providing cost-aligned planning support.

  • Cost alignment with design intent
  • Coordination support for design-build projects
  • Review of design documents for estimation
  • Planning support through design phases

Budget Planning

Construction Budget Planning

Structured cost planning to support budgeting and informed project decisions. We help contractors establish a financial framework for their projects during pre-construction, making it easier to manage scope, expenses, and contingencies.

  • Pre-construction budget development
  • Cost framework organized by project phase
  • Supports owner and lender reporting
  • Useful for project approval and financing stages

Plan Review

Blueprint & Plan Review

Review of available project drawings to support quantity calculations and estimation accuracy. We examine your architectural, structural, and other available plans to ensure our estimates are grounded in your actual project documentation.

  • Architectural and structural drawing review
  • Identification of scope and specification details
  • Supports takeoff and estimation accuracy
  • Document-based, not assumption-based estimating
