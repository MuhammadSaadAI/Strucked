Who We Are

Struked LLC — Pre-Construction Services

Struked LLC is a construction cost estimation company dedicated to helping general contractors and subcontractors make informed project decisions through accurate, reliable estimates.

Our services include detailed material takeoffs and design-build support, helping construction professionals improve planning, manage costs, and approach projects with confidence.

We work with a focused methodology — reviewing your project drawings carefully, applying precise quantity calculations, and delivering organized estimates that give you a clear picture of your project's cost requirements.

Our Approach

Practical Estimation for Real Projects
